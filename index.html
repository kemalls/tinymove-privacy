TinyMove

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the TinyMove iPhone app and this policy page handle information.

Information on your device

TinyMove works without a TinyMove account. When you set up the app or use it, TinyMove saves your onboarding answers, movement preferences and limitations, workout history and feedback, progress and rewards, and reminder settings in its local storage. The app uses this information to choose suitable exercises, save progress, and schedule reminders. The current release of TinyMove does not send this information to us or use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Reminders

If you enable reminders, TinyMove asks for notification permission and schedules them on your device. You can turn reminders off in TinyMove under Profile or revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Apple subscriptions

If you buy TinyMove Plus, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. TinyMove uses Apple’s StoreKit to check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases you request. TinyMove does not receive your payment card information. Apple handles information you provide to the App Store under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

TinyMove keeps your local information until you clear it. To erase your TinyMove preferences, workout history, progress, and reminders from the app, open Profile and choose Reset Local Data. This does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase records held by Apple. You can manage your subscription in Apple’s subscription settings.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. This page does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Questions and updates

For general questions about this policy, you can reach the developer through the TinyMove privacy repository. Issues are public. Please leave personal information and Apple purchase details out of your post.

Any updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
